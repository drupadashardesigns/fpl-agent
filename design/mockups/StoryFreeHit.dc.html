Why not GW14, the tallest bar

GW14 leads GW18 by 0.8 points, which is less than this model can tell apart. Playing it early also leaves nothing in hand for the festive run, when GW17, GW18 and GW19 land within seven days (deadlines 26 Dec, 29 Dec and 1 Jan).

How this was worked out

For each gameweek, the planner builds the best eleven your £101.1m budget allows, keeping £16.5m back for a cheap bench, and compares it with the best eleven from your current squad. The captain counts double in both. Projections come from the v0 model.

How sure we are

Not very, and that is why the call is to wait. The v0 model has no rotation model yet, which matters most in the festive weeks. Treat any gap under 1 point as a tie. Play it early only if three or more starters are out in the same week.
