# Chip

Tags name what kind of story a headline is; pills carry short facts.

- **Tags** (`label-strong`, `radius-pill`) take one tint by meaning: `green-tint` looking back, `orange-tint` a decision or a price rise, `blue-tint` fixtures and data, `red-tint` a fall or injury on a player you own. Text uses the matching ink token.
- **Pills** (`pill` style) hold captain, vice, money left. The captain pill is the only orange one; on the navy call card the others use `navy-raised`.
- One tag per headline card, top left.
